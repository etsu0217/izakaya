SAKE & SEAFOOD

Good food, no fuss —

take all the time you like.

Izakaya R is located in Nishinari-ku, Osaka.
It is a neighbourhood izakaya where locals and visitors to Nishinari alike
can drop in without a second thought.

We cook with fresh ingredients and serve a wide range of small plates made for drinking,
in a room we work hard to keep warm and easy to settle into.

Come alone for a quick drink, bring friends for dinner, or book the room for a party — all are welcome.

Enjoy good food and good drink in the friendly atmosphere that only Nishinari has,
and take your time over it.
All of us here look forward to welcoming you.

CONCEPT

The Izakaya R Way

Straight to the point: good fish, good sake.

Izakaya R hangs its noren in a back street of Nishinari.
Fish bought fresh every morning is served as sashimi, fried, or in a pot — whichever way brings out the most of the ingredient.

Beyond our signature boat platters and spiny lobster dishes, we are proud of how our sake and shochu, gathered from across Japan, match the food. The real thing, without any of the formality.
